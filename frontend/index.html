About FinNews AI

FinNews AI is built to democratize financial literacy. Traditional market commentary is often dense with technical language—such as basis points, yield curve inversions, quantitative tightening, and CapEx multiples. FinNews AI bridges the gap between institutional analysis and the everyday investor or student.
